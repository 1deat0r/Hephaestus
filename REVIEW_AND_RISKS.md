# Design review and risk register
Version 1.2 · 30 September 2026

## What this review is

This is an author-led multidisciplinary review by the same assistant that prepared the specification, plus the automated package/reference checks reported separately. No independent human board, separate expert-agent fleet, external replication, or runtime benchmark was performed. A confidence score such as 9.5/10 would not substitute for those missing forms of validation.

## Corrections incorporated

REV-01 — Product framing: the original architecture began with a hypothesis. The normative system now starts from an authorized broad goal and must originate opportunities and hypotheses. Discovery itself has evaluation metrics.

REV-02 — Jev semantics: the earlier conversation incorrectly grouped Jev with deterministic reasoning. It is now an optional probabilistic decision adapter and cannot approve scientific truth, permissions, proofs, novelty, or promotion.

REV-03 — Falsification semantics: missing a numerical engineering target does not refute a mechanism; noisy or underpowered data may remain inconclusive. Result dimensions and interval-based interpretation are separated.

REV-04 — Novelty: graph absence and zero-hit retrieval do not establish novelty. Claim charts report search scope and near-matches; legal determinations are not automated.

REV-05 — Search overconfidence: universal novelty-times-utility scores and fixed huge agent counts were replaced by gates, a multi-objective archive, bounded allocation, and matched-baseline evaluation.

REV-06 — Physical validity: a simulator validates its modeled behavior under assumptions, not a physical invention automatically. Domain packs require validity envelopes and independent transfer evidence.

REV-07 — Decomposition overhead: nano-harnesses compile to bounded task contracts rather than a newly compiled native program or an LLM call for every tiny step. Tasks may remain coarse or be batched.

REV-08 — Scientific leakage: discovery, screening, and confirmation are separated. Protected evaluators and sealed workloads are inaccessible to candidate workers; changed endpoints are versioned.

REV-09 — Runtime truthfulness: replay guarantees are scoped; ambiguous non-idempotent effects are reconciled. No universal exactly-once or deterministic-model-output guarantee is claimed.

REV-10 — Research grounding: co-scientist's introduction is dated 19 February 2025. MC-NEST has a 2025 preprint and is not described as newly published this week. AgentIdeaBench exploration gains are not restated as an originality gain.

REV-11 — Early-stage discovery: ideas with no presently accessible discriminator are archived as exploratory or blocked, not deleted. This preserves high-risk research without overstating test readiness.

REV-12 — Build realism: a local software-first vertical slice precedes optional graph infrastructure, GPU acceleration, broad physical domains, and self-improvement.

## Readiness disposition

Ready to begin staged implementation of the declared computational scope. Not validated as an invention-producing system. Runtime acceptance tests, security testing, real provider contract tests, statistical-method qualification, and prospective invention evaluation remain required.

Any newly discovered critical contradiction blocks the affected task or release scope until resolved. The absence of a detected issue in this author-led review does not mean none exists.

## Version 1.2 mandatory self-improvement

The user's explicit requirement adds R-115–R-119 and moves R-070–R-072 to M3. The core loop must autonomously generate improvements from mission evidence, evaluate them under protected matched-budget fresh tests, deploy qualified permitted changes, persist learning for subsequent missions, and monitor/roll back regressions. M6 retains optional advanced learning and new domains. Schema 1.2 adds an improvement-candidate record, a synthetic unexecuted proposal and reference metadata checks.

No actual improvement, training, deployment, cross-mission learning or rollback has been executed. Real benefit, method qualification, continual-learning stability, hidden feedback leakage and recovery behavior remain runtime evaluation obligations. Retain the incumbent when evidence is inconclusive; no monotonically increasing capability is claimed. The prior 1.1 specification, schemas, fixtures and validation receipts are archived.
